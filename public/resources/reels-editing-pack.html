Reels Editing Pack

Starter guide and preset recipes to make your Reels, TikToks, and Shorts pop fast.

The 3 Second Rule

If a viewer is not hooked in the first 3 seconds, they scroll. Open with one of:
  1. A surprising face or reaction
  2. A bold text claim ("I made KES 15k in one week with Canva")
  3. A close up of the end result (a finished cake, a finished poster)
  4. A quick zoom cut that creates motion

Export Settings

  • Resolution: 1080 x 1920 (9:16 vertical)
  • Frame rate: 30fps (or 60fps for fast cuts)
  • Bitrate: high, but under 15MB for WhatsApp
  • Format: MP4 (H.264)

Safe Zones

  • Keep faces and key text out of the top 15% (username area) and bottom 20% (caption, like buttons).
  • Put big headlines in the middle third of the screen.

3 Color Preset Recipes

Warm Vibe (food, lifestyle): Warmth +12, Saturation +8, Contrast +6, Highlights -10, Shadows +8, Orange hue shifted slightly toward red.
Cool Tech (coding, AI, gadgets): Temperature -8, Tint toward magenta +4, Contrast +10, Sharpness +15, Blues boosted in shadows.
Neo Brutalist Pop (education, marketing): Contrast +14, Vibrance +18, Saturation +6, Blacks crushed, Highlights -8, add a slight vignette at 18%.

Audio Tips

  • Voiceover should sit at -6dB to -8dB.
  • Background music at -18dB to -22dB when someone is speaking.
  • Cut music on the beat: zoom into waveforms and align cuts to the spikes.
  • Use 1 frame of silence at the very start to avoid awkward pops.

5 Must Know Transitions

  1. Hard cut on beat: simplest and punchiest. Cut exactly when the beat drops.
  2. Zoom punch: keyframe from 100% to 115% scale across 4 frames, then cut.
  3. Whip pan: quick horizontal blur between shots. Add motion blur.
  4. Mask reveal: use a shape mask to reveal the next clip underneath.
  5. Jump cut with text pop: cut 1 to 2 seconds forward and drop a bold text sticker.

Caption Style We Love

  • Font: bold sans (Impact, Anton, Bebas Neue, or CapCut "Bold" fonts)
  • Size: fill about 60 percent of screen width, 2 lines max
  • Outline: 4 to 6px black, no shadow or a tiny drop shadow
  • Color: white words with one accent word in lime or pink
  • Pacing: show 1 to 3 words at a time, synced to your voice

Checklist Before Posting

  • Hook in first 3 seconds
  • Captions burnt in (85 percent of people watch without sound)
  • Text inside safe zone
  • Ends with one clear CTA ("Follow for more", "Comment YES", "Link in bio")
  • First 3 seconds plays smoothly even on bad network (no long fades in)
