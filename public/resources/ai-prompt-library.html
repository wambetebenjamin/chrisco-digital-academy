120 Prompts Starter Pack

AI Prompt Library

For CHRISCO learners. Copy, tweak, and make these your own. Great prompts = great output.

Rule of thumb: give the AI a ROLE, a TASK, a FORMAT, and a TONE. Be specific about audience and length.

Learning & Studying

Explain like I am 15
Explain [topic] to me like I am 15 years old. Use one real world African example. Then give me 3 quick check questions at the end to test my understanding.
Socratic tutor
You are my patient tutor. I am learning [subject]. Do NOT give me the answer directly. Ask me guiding questions that lead me to figure it out myself. I am stuck on: [paste problem].
Make a study plan
Create a 14 day study plan for learning [skill]. I have 30 minutes a day, mobile only. Break it into bite sized daily tasks with one tiny win per day. End each day with a 3 question quiz.

Writing & Copywriting

Hook generator
Write 10 scroll stopping opening hooks for a [platform: TikTok/Reels/Instagram] video about [topic]. Target audience is [describe]. Keep each under 12 words. Make them punchy and specific to African youth.
Rewrite for clarity
Rewrite the text below to be shorter, clearer, and more conversational. Keep the meaning. Aim for a grade 8 reading level. Use short sentences. Add one strong call to action at the end.

[paste text]

Coding Help

Debug with explanation
I am getting this error in [language]: [paste error]. Here is my code: [paste code]. 1) Explain in simple English what is wrong. 2) Show the fixed code. 3) Teach me how to spot this bug myself next time.
Build small project
Help me build a tiny [app type] using [tools]. I am a beginner. Give me step by step instructions, one step per message. Wait for me to finish each step before giving the next. The app should do [describe].

Design & Content

Color palette
Suggest 3 neo brutalist color palettes for a youth brand in [niche]. For each, give hex codes, what each color should be used for (background, text, accent), and why the combo works for Gen Z Africans.
Canva brief
Write a Canva design brief for a [type of graphic] promoting [offer]. Target audience: [who]. Tone: bold, loud, confident. Include headline, sub headline, CTA, and suggested imagery. Keep it under 15 words of copy total.

Freelancing & Business

Cold DM opener
Write 5 short WhatsApp DM openers I can send to a potential [niche] client in Kenya. Do not sound salesy. Be warm, specific, and lead with one genuine observation about their business. End with a soft question.
Rate card rationale
I am a freelance [skill] beginner in Kenya. Help me price my first 3 packages (basic, standard, premium) in KES. For each, list exactly what is included and one reason the client should pick it. Keep prices realistic for beginners.

Video & Social Media

Short video script
Write a 30 second vertical video script about [topic]. Structure: hook (0 to 3s), value (3 to 22s), CTA (22 to 30s). Include on screen text cues and B roll suggestions. Speak like a cool older sibling, not a teacher.
Pro tip: always add "Do not use emojis" or "Use simple language" if the output feels too corporate.
