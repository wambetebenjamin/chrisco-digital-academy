4. Color Palette

Pick 4 colors max: 1 main, 1 secondary, 1 accent, 1 neutral.

Main:  Used for logo, big buttons

Secondary:  Used for headings, links

Accent:  Used for highlights, stickers

Neutral ink:  Used for body text, borders

Tip: change the hex codes to your own. Keep high contrast against white or cream.

5. Typography

Body / paragraph font:

Both free in Canva and Google Fonts. Pair one bold chunky font with one clean sans.

6. Logo Directions

Sketch 3 very different ideas on paper first. Do NOT open Canva yet.

  • Wordmark only (the name styled cool)
  • Icon + wordmark (a small symbol next to the name)
  • Emblem (logo inside a shape like a circle or badge)

7. Do and Do Not

Do:

  • Keep the logo readable even at tiny size (profile pic)
  • Use 2 fonts max
  • Stick to the same 4 colors everywhere
  • Make at least one black and one white version

Do not:

  • Use drop shadows on everything
  • Use more than one "fancy" font
  • Copy another brand exactly
  • Put text over busy photos without a solid plate behind it

8. Launch Checklist

  • Logo in PNG (transparent) and SVG or high res JPG
  • Profile picture version (cropped tight, works at 100px)
  • Cover banner for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp Business
  • 3 Canva templates: flyer, quote card, product post
  • One page brand cheat sheet with colors and fonts saved
