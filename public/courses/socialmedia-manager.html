Course Description

Learn how to professionally manage social media accounts for businesses, brands, and creators. This practical course covers strategy, content creation, scheduling, community management, analytics, and running paid campaigns. Perfect for those who want to become Social Media Managers or grow their own brand — with a strong focus on Kenyan and African markets.

Who This Course Is For

  • Youth who want to work as Social Media Managers (freelance or employed)
  • Business owners who want to manage their own accounts professionally
  • Anyone interested in digital marketing and content creation
  • Complete beginners — no prior experience required

Learning Outcomes

  • Develop full social media strategies for any brand
  • Create and schedule content across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn & WhatsApp
  • Manage communities and engage with audiences effectively
  • Analyze performance and make data-driven decisions
  • Run small paid campaigns and report results to clients
  • Land freelance clients or manage accounts professionally

Tools You Will Need

  • Free: Canva, CapCut, Meta Business Suite, ChatGPT
  • Free Tier: Later, Buffer, or Meta Scheduler
  • Analytics: Platform native insights + Google Analytics
  • Smartphone + Laptop + Internet

📅 Detailed 8-Week Syllabus

Week 1: Introduction to Social Media Management

  • What a Social Media Manager actually does
  • Overview of major platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X)
  • Understanding Kenyan & African audience behavior
  • Setting up professional business accounts
Weekly Task: Audit 3 local business social media accounts and identify improvements

Week 2: Social Media Strategy Development

  • Creating a complete social media strategy document
  • Defining goals, target audience & KPIs
  • Content pillars and content calendar planning
  • Competitor analysis
Weekly Task: Create a 30-day content strategy for a chosen brand

Week 3: Content Creation & Curation

  • Creating scroll-stopping visuals and videos
  • Writing captions that drive engagement
  • Reels, Stories, Carousels & TikTok trends
  • Content curation and repurposing

Week 4: Scheduling, Posting & Tools

  • Using scheduling tools effectively
  • Best times to post on each platform
  • Batch creating content
  • Hashtag strategy and trending topics

Week 5: Community Management & Engagement

  • Responding to comments and DMs professionally
  • Building and managing online communities
  • Handling negative feedback and crises
  • Running contests, giveaways & engagement campaigns

Week 6: Analytics, Reporting & Optimization

  • Reading and understanding platform analytics
  • Creating professional monthly reports for clients
  • A/B testing and optimization
  • ROI tracking

Week 7: Paid Social Media & Advertising

  • Running Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)
  • Creating ad sets and targeting
  • Budget management for small businesses
  • Retargeting and lookalike audiences

Week 8: Client Management & Freelancing

  • How to get clients as a Social Media Manager
  • Pricing your services (monthly retainers)
  • Contracts, onboarding & reporting to clients
  • Building long-term client relationships

Final Project (Must Complete)

Manage a real or simulated social media account for 4 weeks. Create a full strategy, post consistently, run one small ad campaign, and deliver a professional monthly report with results and recommendations.
