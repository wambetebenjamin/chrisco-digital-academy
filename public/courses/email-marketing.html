Email Marketing

8-Week Complete Course • Build Your Most Valuable Asset — Your Email List

Week 1 of 8 • Start Today

Course Description

Learn how to build, grow, and monetize an email list — one of the most powerful and profitable digital assets. This practical course teaches you how to attract subscribers, write emails that people actually open and buy from, set up automated sequences, and turn your list into a consistent source of income. Perfect for freelancers, business owners, and content creators in Africa.

Who This Course Is For

  • Youth who want to build a direct line to their audience
  • Business owners and freelancers who want to sell more
  • Content creators and bloggers
  • Anyone who wants to earn through email without relying on social media algorithms
  • Complete beginners — no prior experience needed

Learning Outcomes

  • Build and grow a targeted email list from scratch
  • Write high-converting emails and subject lines
  • Create automated email sequences (welcome, nurture, sales)
  • Segment your audience for better results
  • Comply with email marketing laws
  • Monetize your list through offers and promotions

Tools You Will Need

  • Free Tier: Mailchimp, Brevo (Sendinblue), ConvertKit, or MailerLite
  • Free: Canva (for visuals), ChatGPT (for writing help)
  • Google Forms or landing page tool
  • Internet connection

📅 Detailed 8-Week Syllabus

Week 1: Introduction to Email Marketing

  • Why email marketing still works in 2026
  • The power of owning your audience (vs social media)
  • Overview of email marketing platforms
  • Setting realistic goals for your list
Weekly Task: Choose your email platform and create your account

Week 2: Building Your Email List

  • Creating irresistible lead magnets (freebies)
  • Setting up opt-in forms and landing pages
  • Where to promote your signup (social, WhatsApp, website)
  • Growing your list ethically and fast
Weekly Task: Create your first lead magnet and signup form

Week 3: Writing Emails That Get Opened

  • Subject line formulas that boost open rates
  • Writing engaging email body content
  • Personalization and storytelling in emails
  • Best practices for mobile-friendly emails

Week 4: Email Sequences & Automation

  • Creating welcome sequences
  • Nurture sequences that build trust
  • Sales sequences that convert
  • Setting up automation workflows

Week 5: Segmentation & Personalization

  • Why segmentation matters
  • Segmenting your list by behavior and interests
  • Advanced personalization techniques
  • Improving deliverability

Week 6: Compliance, Deliverability & Best Practices

  • Email laws (GDPR, CAN-SPAM, Kenya Data Protection Act)
  • How to stay compliant and avoid spam folders
  • Double opt-in vs single opt-in
  • Cleaning your list regularly

Week 7: Monetizing Your Email List

  • Promoting your own products and services
  • Affiliate marketing via email
  • Launching digital products through email
  • Measuring ROI from email campaigns

Week 8: Scaling & Advanced Email Strategies

  • Advanced automation and broadcast strategies
  • A/B testing emails
  • Case studies of successful email marketers
  • Creating your long-term email marketing plan

Final Project (Must Complete)

Build a complete email marketing system: Create a lead magnet, set up your signup form, grow your list to at least 100 subscribers, and launch one automated sequence (welcome or sales). Present your results and open/click rates.
