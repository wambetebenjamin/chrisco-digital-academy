About this course

Communication is the number-one skill that separates leaders from followers. In this course you learn to speak with the authority, clarity and presence of a top CEO — so people listen, trust and follow you.

What you'll learn

1

Executive presence & mindset

Build the inner confidence and self-image of a leader before you say a single word.

2

Speaking with clarity & authority

Structure your thoughts and speak in clear, powerful, concise sentences.

3

Body language & vocal power

Use posture, eye contact, pace and tone to command any room.

4

Public speaking & presentations

Plan and deliver talks and pitches that move people to action.

5

Persuasive, confident messaging

Frame your ideas so they land — influence without pressure.

6

Handling difficult conversations

Give feedback, say no, and handle conflict with calm authority.

7

Communicating on camera & online

Show up powerfully on video calls, livestreams and social.

8

Final project — Deliver a CEO-level talk

Record and deliver a 3-minute leadership talk and get feedback.

By the end

Outcome: You will speak, present and lead with the confidence and polish of the top 1%.
