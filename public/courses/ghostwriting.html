What Is Ghostwriting?

Ghostwriting is the professional practice of creating written content that is officially credited to another person. From memoirs and business books to LinkedIn posts and keynote speeches, ghostwriters give voice to leaders, influencers, and entrepreneurs who have ideas—but not the time or craft to write them.

Unlike traditional authorship, ghostwriting is a service business. You are paid for your skill, speed, discretion, and ability to capture someone else's voice. Elite ghostwriters can earn six figures per book, while agency ghostwriters build scalable retainers across multiple clients.

Why This Course Exists

Most writers try to "make it" by blogging for pennies or chasing vanity publishing. This master-course treats ghostwriting as a high-ticket craft and business. By the end, you won't just write well—you'll know how to find clients, price projects, match voices, and build a premium ghostwriting practice.
