Course Description

Learn the high-income skill of ghostwriting — writing books, articles, LinkedIn posts, speeches, and social media content for busy professionals, entrepreneurs, and thought leaders who get all the credit. This practical course teaches you how to capture someone else’s voice, research effectively, deliver professional work, and build a lucrative freelance ghostwriting business. One of the best-paid writing skills in the digital economy.

Who This Course Is For

  • Youth who are good at writing and want to earn premium rates
  • Aspiring freelance writers looking for high-paying clients
  • Anyone interested in writing books or content for others
  • Complete beginners who want to master professional writing

Learning Outcomes

  • Write in someone else’s voice and style convincingly
  • Conduct effective research and interviews
  • Deliver professional ghostwritten books, articles, and posts
  • Find and land high-paying ghostwriting clients
  • Manage projects, contracts, and revisions professionally
  • Build a sustainable and profitable ghostwriting business

Tools You Will Need

  • Free: Google Docs, Grammarly, ChatGPT/Claude
  • Recommended: Notion or Google Drive for project management
  • Internet + Word processor

📅 Detailed 8-Week Syllabus

Week 1: Introduction to Ghostwriting

  • What is ghostwriting and why it pays well
  • Different types of ghostwriting (books, articles, LinkedIn, speeches, social media)
  • The mindset and ethics of a ghostwriter
  • Realistic income potential for ghostwriters
Weekly Task: Research 5 successful ghostwriters and study their work

Week 2: Finding Your Voice & Capturing Others’ Voices

  • How to analyze and match someone’s writing style
  • Techniques for capturing tone, personality, and rhythm
  • Exercises to practice writing in different voices
  • Working with client briefs and guidelines
Weekly Task: Rewrite 3 pieces of content in 3 different voices

Week 3: Research & Interviewing Skills

  • Effective research methods for ghostwriting
  • How to conduct powerful interviews
  • Asking the right questions and digging deeper
  • Organizing research for easy writing

Week 4: Writing Books & Long-Form Content

  • Structuring and outlining a book or long article
  • Chapter-by-chapter writing process
  • Maintaining consistency across long projects
  • Working with outlines and client feedback

Week 5: Ghostwriting for Social Media & Thought Leadership

  • Writing LinkedIn posts and personal branding content
  • Creating Twitter/X threads and Instagram captions
  • Writing speeches and presentations
  • Short-form ghostwriting that sounds authentic

Week 6: Finding Clients & Getting Hired

  • Where to find ghostwriting clients (Upwork, LinkedIn, referrals)
  • Creating a strong ghostwriting portfolio
  • Writing winning proposals and pitches
  • Networking with authors, CEOs, and thought leaders

Week 7: Pricing, Contracts & Project Management

  • How to price ghostwriting projects (per word, per project, retainers)
  • Creating professional contracts and agreements
  • Managing revisions and client expectations
  • Protecting yourself legally and financially

Week 8: Building a Sustainable Ghostwriting Business

  • Scaling from one client to multiple clients
  • Creating systems and processes
  • Case studies of successful ghostwriters
  • Your 6-month ghostwriting business plan

Final Project (Must Complete)

Complete a full ghostwriting project: Write either 3 LinkedIn articles or 1 full chapter of a book (minimum 2,000 words) for a real or simulated client. Include research notes, outline, and final polished version. Present your work professionally.
