Course Description

Learn how to earn money online by promoting other people’s products and earning commissions (up to 50% per sale). No need for inventory, customer service, or your own website at the beginning. This course focuses on beginner-friendly strategies perfect for Kenyan youth to earn in USD using platforms like ClickBank, Amazon Associates, Digistore24, and local Kenyan affiliate programs.

Who This Course Is For

  • Youth who want to earn passive or semi-passive income in dollars
  • Students and side-hustlers with smartphone + internet
  • Anyone tired of low-paying local jobs
  • Complete beginners — zero experience required

Learning Outcomes

  • Join and promote high-paying affiliate programs
  • Build review websites, blogs, and social media campaigns
  • Drive free and paid traffic that converts
  • Track earnings and scale to full-time income
  • Create multiple income streams

Tools You Will Need (Mostly Free)

  • Free: Canva, ChatGPT, Google Sites / Carrd
  • Free Tier: WordPress.com, Linktree, Bitly
  • Recommended: ClickBank, Amazon Associates, Digistore24
  • WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok

📅 Detailed 8-Week Syllabus

Week 1: Introduction & Program Selection

  • How affiliate marketing really works
  • Best affiliate programs for beginners in Africa (ClickBank, Amazon, WarriorPlus, etc.)
  • Niche selection that converts well in Kenya
  • Setting up your affiliate accounts
Weekly Task: Join 5 affiliate programs and choose your main niche

Week 2: Content Creation for Affiliates

  • Writing honest product review content
  • Creating comparison posts and “Top 5” lists
  • Using ChatGPT to write high-converting copy
  • Designing banners and graphics in Canva
Weekly Task: Write 3 product reviews with affiliate links

Week 3: Building Your Affiliate Platform

  • Free website builders (Carrd, Google Sites, WordPress)
  • Link-in-bio pages for mobile traffic
  • Email list building with free tools
  • Legal & disclosure best practices

Week 4: Free Traffic Strategies

  • Instagram & TikTok affiliate marketing
  • WhatsApp status & group strategies
  • Facebook Groups and Pinterest
  • Content marketing & SEO basics

Week 5: Paid Traffic & Scaling

  • Running low-budget Facebook & Instagram ads
  • Native advertising platforms
  • Budget management for positive ROI
  • Retargeting strategies
Weekly Task: Run your first small paid campaign (KSh 1,000 budget)

Week 6: Email Marketing for Affiliates

  • Building an email list
  • Writing email sequences that sell
  • Free email tools (Mailchimp, Brevo)
  • Automation setup

Week 7: Advanced Techniques & Automation

  • Creating your own digital products to promote
  • Building funnels and upsells
  • Tracking conversions and analytics
  • Managing multiple offers

Week 8: Scaling & Long-term Success

  • Case studies of successful African affiliate marketers
  • Tax & payment withdrawal tips (PayPal, Payoneer)
  • Building a sustainable business
  • Portfolio & client pitching (if offering services)

Final Project (Must Complete)

Build a complete affiliate marketing system: Choose a niche, create 5 promotional contents (reviews/posts), set up a link page or simple website, drive traffic, and generate your first commission(s) during the course.
