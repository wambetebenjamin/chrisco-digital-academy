Course Description

This hands-on course teaches you how to market businesses, products, and services online using Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, and Google. Special focus on Kenyan and African markets — how to run effective campaigns with small budgets (as low as KSh 500 per day) and get real customers.

By the end of the course, you will be able to manage social media and run paid ads for clients or your own business.

Who This Course Is For

  • Youth in Kenya & Africa wanting remote digital jobs
  • Small business owners who want to grow online
  • Students and graduates looking for freelance income
  • Complete beginners — no prior experience needed

Learning Outcomes

  • Create high-converting content for social media
  • Run profitable Facebook & Instagram ads
  • Build and manage WhatsApp Business funnels
  • Measure results and optimize campaigns
  • Earn as a freelance Digital Marketer

Tools You Will Need (Mostly Free)

  • Free: Meta Business Suite, Canva, WhatsApp Business
  • Free Tier: Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel
  • Recommended: CapCut, Later or Buffer (free plan)
  • Smartphone + Internet

📅 Detailed 8-Week Syllabus

Week 1: Digital Marketing Foundations

  • Understanding customer journey in African markets
  • Buyer psychology and pain points
  • Overview of all major platforms (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp, Google)
  • Setting up Meta Business Manager
Weekly Task: Choose a business (yours or local) and create buyer personas

Week 2: Content Creation Mastery

  • Creating scroll-stopping visuals with Canva
  • Writing captions that sell
  • Content types that work best in Kenya (Reels, Stories, Carousels)
  • Branding & visual identity
Weekly Task: Create 7 days of content for your chosen business
Open Canva

Week 3: Organic Social Media Growth

  • Instagram & TikTok growth strategies
  • Facebook Groups & Community building
  • WhatsApp Business catalog & broadcasts
  • Posting schedule for maximum reach

Week 4: Facebook & Instagram Ads

  • Setting up ads account and pixel
  • Targeting Kenyan audience effectively
  • Creating high-converting ad copies & creatives
  • Budget management (KSh 300–2000/day)
Weekly Task: Run your first small test ad (KSh 500 budget)

Week 5: Sales Funnels & WhatsApp Marketing

  • Building lead generation funnels
  • WhatsApp automation & catalogs
  • Follow-up sequences that convert
  • Turning followers into paying customers

Week 6: Google & Local SEO

  • Google My Business setup
  • Local SEO for Kenyan businesses
  • Google Ads basics
  • Review generation strategies

Week 7: Analytics, Optimization & Reporting

  • Reading Meta & Google Analytics
  • A/B testing creatives & audiences
  • ROI calculation
  • Creating professional client reports

Week 8: Freelancing & Scaling

  • How to get clients locally and on Upwork
  • Pricing your services
  • Building a portfolio
  • Managing multiple clients

Final Project (Must Complete)

Run a complete 14-day digital marketing campaign for a real local business or your own product. Deliver a full report showing reach, engagement, leads, and sales (if any).
