Master the skill of persuasive writing that turns readers into buyers. Perfect for freelancers, business owners, and anyone who wants to earn by writing.

Learning Outcomes

  • Write persuasive copy using proven frameworks (AIDA, PAS, etc.)

📅 Detailed 8-Week Syllabus

Week 1: Introduction to Copywriting

  • What is copywriting and why it matters
  • Difference between content writing and copywriting
  • The psychology of persuasion
  • Overview of different types of copy (sales, email, ads, social)
Weekly Task: Analyze 5 sales pages or ads and note what makes them effective

Week 2: Core Copywriting Frameworks

  • AIDA Formula (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
  • PAS Framework (Problem, Agitate, Solution)
  • Before-After-Bridge method
  • Writing powerful headlines and hooks
Weekly Task: Rewrite 3 weak headlines into powerful ones

Week 3: Writing Sales Pages & Landing Pages

  • Structure of a high-converting sales page
  • Writing bullet points that sell
  • Creating irresistible offers
  • Call-to-Action (CTA) mastery

Week 4: Email Copywriting

  • Writing welcome sequences and sales emails
  • Subject lines that get opened
  • Storytelling in emails
  • Follow-up sequences that convert

Week 5: Social Media & Ad Copy

  • Writing scroll-stopping social media captions
  • Facebook & Instagram ad copy that converts
  • Short-form copy for Reels, TikTok & Stories
  • Creating urgency and scarcity

Week 6: Product Descriptions & E-commerce Copy

  • Writing benefit-driven product descriptions
  • SEO-friendly copy for online stores
  • Copy for Jumia, Shopify, and local e-commerce
  • Using sensory language

Week 7: AI Tools for Copywriters

  • Using ChatGPT and Claude effectively for copy
  • Prompt engineering for better results
  • Editing and humanizing AI-generated copy
  • Ethical use of AI in professional copywriting

Week 8: Building a Copywriting Business

  • Creating a professional copywriting portfolio
  • Finding clients (Upwork, cold outreach, local businesses)
  • Pricing your services
  • Contracts, proposals & client management

Final Project (Must Complete)

Write a complete sales page (or 5-email sequence) for a real or fictional product/service. Include headline, subheadlines, bullets, story, offer, and strong CTA. Present it professionally in your portfolio.
