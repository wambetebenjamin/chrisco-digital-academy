### 2.0 Your Project

**2.1 Project Summary — brief description (Max 500 words)**

> CHRISCO Digital Academy is a youth digital and economic-empowerment programme under CHRISCO Youth Aflame. It
> equips young people with practical, income-generating skills through a live online platform of **19 hands-on
> courses**. Alongside technical skills — Python and software engineering with AI, web development, graphic design,
> content creation and AI video storytelling — it now teaches the high-value business and empowerment skills young
> people need to earn and lead: **communication (speaking like a CEO), negotiation, personal branding, AI literacy,
> networking, and a full sales & marketing course.** Each course ends with a real, sellable project.
>
> In Jinja and Buikwe, thousands of school leavers and unemployed youth have talent and ambition but no access to
> affordable, practical training, no equipment to learn on, and often no internet at home. This project establishes a
> **Youth Digital & Economic-Empowerment Hub** — a primary centre in **Jinja** and a second training site in
> **Buikwe** — where local youth access the CHRISCO curriculum for free.
>
> MTN Foundation's **in-kind** support (laptops, tablets, a projector, a Wi-Fi router with one year of internet,
> power backup and furniture) turns the ready-made platform into physical learning spaces. Learners are taught
> **online and in person**, complete a portfolio project, and are guided into freelancing, online work and small
> digital businesses. Crucially, the project also teaches learners two things that compound the impact: **how to
> build their own websites** (so they can earn and serve local businesses), and **financial literacy / money
> management** — so the income they earn is saved, reinvested and multiplied, not lost.
>
> To reach youth who have no internet at home, the project will **develop a lightweight offline study app** that
> lets learners download and read the course **PDF handbooks and books offline** on the donated tablets — so learning
> continues after they leave the hub. CHRISCO is also producing **study books and training videos** for every course,
> which become the reusable content library for the app.
>
> Beyond the hub, the founder will **take the training into church settings and schools** (where permitted) to speak
> to youth about branding themselves, using their skills, and creating opportunity — extending reach far beyond the
> physical centre.
>
> The hub runs rolling 8–12 week cohorts. In the first three months we set up equipment, train local facilitators,
> launch the offline app, and graduate the first cohort of at least 200 youth — with a deliberate focus on young
> women, out-of-school youth and persons with disabilities. Because the platform, curriculum, books and videos already
> exist, every donated device converts directly into a learner seat and real training days.
>
> *Live platform (project evidence): 🔴 [your deployed URL — see Part D].*

**2.2 Project Objectives — how will this funding improve lives? (Max 250 words)**

> The funding removes the biggest barriers to skilling in Jinja and Buikwe — equipment, connectivity and access. It
> will enable the project to:
> 1. **Open free, equipped learning hubs** where at least 200 youth per year gain practical digital and business
>    skills they could not otherwise afford.
> 2. **Turn skills into income.** Every course ends in a sellable output (a website, brand kit, content plan or
>    freelancing profile), so graduates earn — reducing youth unemployment.
> 3. **Teach money management.** Learners are trained in financial literacy so the income they make is saved,
>    budgeted and reinvested — the impact keeps growing, not just a one-off.
> 4. **Teach website-building** so youth can serve local businesses and create their own online income.
> 5. **Reach the offline majority.** A simple offline app lets learners study the PDF books on donated tablets
>    without internet.
> 6. **Prioritise the underserved** — young women, out-of-school youth and persons with disabilities.
> 7. **Extend into churches and schools**, speaking to youth about branding and opportunity.
>
> In short, the equipment converts existing curriculum, books and videos into real training, real income, and
> lasting financial skills.

**2.3 Project Beneficiaries — number of people to benefit (Max 250 words)**

> **Direct beneficiaries (first 3 months / first cohorts): at least 200 youth** across Jinja and Buikwe:
> - 110 young women and 90 young men
> - 180 youth (aged 18–35)
> - 15 persons with disabilities (hub and devices set up for inclusive access)
>
> **What "benefit" means:** 200 youth gain hands-on skills and a completed portfolio project; a conservative 60%
> (≈120) begin earning income through freelancing, content creation, website-building or digital services within six
> months, and all are trained to manage that income.
>
> **Indirect beneficiaries (Year 1): 500+ people.** Graduates mentor peers, support families with new income, and
> serve local businesses needing affordable web, design and marketing work. The offline study app and church/school
> outreach extend reach further. As cohorts rotate every 8–12 weeks, the equipped hubs train new groups continuously.
> 🔴 *Adjust these numbers to the real size of your host sites.*

**2.4 Project Sustainability — how will it continue beyond MTN funding? (Max 250 words)**

> The project is built to outlast the one-time equipment donation:
> 1. **Curriculum, books, videos and platform already exist** and are maintained by CHRISCO Digital Academy at no
>    recurring cost — MTN funds only the physical equipment, which serves cohorts for years.
> 2. **Offline app + content library:** once built, the study app and PDF books work forever with no internet cost,
>    so learning continues cheaply and independently.
> 3. **Train-the-trainer:** local facilitators trained in the first cohort run future cohorts, so delivery isn't
>    dependent on outside staff.
> 4. **Graduates earn and give back.** Learners who begin earning contribute a small voluntary community fee or
>    volunteer as mentors, funding consumables (internet renewal, maintenance) after Year 1.
> 5. **Money-management training** means income created in the community is retained and reinvested locally.
> 6. **Host partnership:** the hubs are embedded within CHRISCO Youth Aflame / 🔴 *[local church, school or CBO in
>    Jinja & Buikwe]*, which provides venue, security and ongoing coordination.

Next.js 16 app; runs cleanly with `npm install && npm run dev`. To give MTN a public link:

**Vercel (free, made for Next.js):**
1. Push this repo to GitHub (already connected).
2. vercel.com → **New Project** → import `wambetebenjamin/chrisco-digital-academy`.
3. Framework auto-detects **Next.js** → **Deploy**. You get `chrisco-digital-academy.vercel.app`.
4. (Optional) env vars for sign-in/dashboard: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`,
