Kibra Youth Network

Empowering Youth, Building Stronger Communities

Helping young people unlock potential creates a brighter future for all. At Kibra Youth Network, we provide mental health support, creative education, life skills, and community-driven opportunities. By equipping youth with knowledge, resilience, and creativity, we inspire transformation that extends beyond Kibera to the world. Together, we turn challenges into stepping stones for growth.
